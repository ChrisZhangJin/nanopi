  <action>

Image reads also record. Never fail the read because recording failed.
    write.rs and edit.rs: at the start of execute, resolve `canonical_key`; if Some, acquire `file_state::path_lock(&key).lock_owned().await` and hold it until the write completes (cross-agent serialization per D-15, extending mutation_key). After a successful write, call `ctx.file_state.update_after_write(&key, new_bytes)`. Do not touch cancellation: a write in progress always completes (D-07). Keep existing error messages and tests passing.
  </action>
